How can we find the volume of a frustum?

Cut a pyramid with a plane parallel to its base. Remove the smaller pyramid at the top. The remaining solid is a frustum.

In these examples, each pyramid has a square base, with its apex directly above the centre of the base.

Volume calculation steps
Do not confuse the heights: use H for the larger pyramid and x for the smaller pyramid. The frustum height is h = H − x. Separating the pieces only makes the model easier to inspect; it does not change the volumes.

Solution order

1. Write a proportion using similar triangles.
2. Find the smaller pyramid height, x.
3. Find the larger pyramid height, H = x + h.
4. Calculate both pyramid volumes.
5. Subtract the smaller volume from the larger volume.

Exact values and rounded values

Use = for exact values, including fractions. Do not round intermediate calculations. Use ≈ only when the final value changes after rounding to 3 significant figures.

All heights are perpendicular heights, not slant heights. Diagrams are schematic and are not necessarily drawn to scale.
